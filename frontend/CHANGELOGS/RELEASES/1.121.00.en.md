## [1.121.00] - 08-10-2026

Version calculated from the frontend daily changelogs (01-09-2026 – 08-10-2026), continuing from baseline `1.101.00` (31-08-2026).

## Methodology

1. **Baseline:** `[31-08-2026]` = `1.101.00` (previous release).
2. **Sections:** `FEAT` (MINOR), `FIX`/`CHORE`/`MODIFIED` (PATCH), `BREAKING` (MAJOR).
3. **Day rule:** the highest type wins once (`BREAKING` > `FEAT` > `FIX`/`CHORE`/`MODIFIED`).
4. **Format:** `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` with 2 digits (`1.121.00`).
5. Full convention: [`README.en.md`](./README.en.md).

Source: `CHANGELOGS/2026/202609_CHANGELOG.ca.md`, `CHANGELOGS/CURRENT_CHANGELOG.ca.md`.

### Normalization note

Before calculating, the changelogs were unified: days with a repeated date were merged into a single block and out-of-convention headers (`PATCH`, `PATH`, `PERF`, `ADDED`, `FIXED`, `CHORE/FEAT`, or text without a header) were reclassified to `FEAT`/`FIX`/`CHORE`/`MODIFIED`. None of these changes alters the already published versions (1.76.00 – 1.101.00).
