## Highlights

- Security: access tokens expire and are revoked on logout or password change; integration users' tokens expire after 15 days.
- Password recovery from the login page and own password change from the profile.
- Person anomalies and insolvency process linked to debt management, with manual moving of the process invoices to delinquent or irrecoverable.
- Virtual Office: PDF and upload of the signed contract of a request, SEPA mandate on sign-up, collection account, payment method and returned receipt on invoices, a person's contracts, consumptions by ID number, card payment of direct-debit invoices and OpenAPI documentation in Catalan.
- Webhooks for invoice payment status and cancelled requests.
- Joint OTP signing of contract, SEPA mandate and invoice.
- VAT calculated on the base of each rate.
- Accounting: settings per exploitation, accounting types, XML/CSV export and separate collections and returns.

## Notes

- **Deploy backend and frontend together.**
- **No new dependencies.** Images are now also published for ARM (`linux/arm64`) (#441).
- **Migrations** (`migrate`):
  - billing `0353`–`0355`
  - contract `0252`
  - coredata `0160`–`0162`
  - documentmanager `0020`
  - integrations `0008`
  - logger `0056`
  - order `0037`
  - pricing `0142`–`0147`
  - statistics `0064`
  - Data migrations:
    - `coredata.0160` creates the four person anomaly types.
    - `coredata.0162` moves `attach_invoices_in_claims` and `attach_extra_docs_in_claims` to the new `claim_attached_documents` ConfigProject, keeping the same behaviour, and deletes the two old ones (#477).
    - `billing.0355` creates the sign-up tariff row in the ACA settings of the exploitations that already have them (#490).
    - `logger.0056` marks old payment method changes caused by a debt block (#508).
    - `order.0037` renames the order reason `canvi_titular` to `change_of_owner` and revises the order types of the `contract_termination` reason (#493).
- **Opscripts** (`manage.py run_pending_scripts`):
  - `statistics/0008_add_user_city_management_report`: registers the "Actions by user and municipality" report (#457).
- **New ConfigProject:**
  - `claim_attached_documents` (`invoices`, `payment_slips`, both comma-separated, or empty): documents attached to debt claim communications (#477). Created by the migration.
- **New environment variables** (all optional):
  - `AUTH_TOKEN_IDLE_HOURS` (default `24`), `AUTH_TOKEN_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default `7`), `AUTH_TOKEN_NON_EXPIRING_USERS`: person sessions and additional integration users (#359).
  - `AUTH_INTEGRATION_TOKEN_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default `15`): expiry of integration users' tokens (#453).
  - `FRONTEND_URL` (default `https://FRONTEND_DOMAIN`) and `PASSWORD_RESET_TIMEOUT` (default `3600` seconds): link in the password recovery email (#513).
  - `EXTRA_SUPERUSER_USERNAMES`: accounts that, besides `customers`, can be superusers (#470).
- **Behaviour changes:**
  - #359: **after deploying, people must log in again once**: their permanent token stops working. Each login creates a session that expires after 24 h of inactivity or 7 days of age, and "Log out" or a password change revokes it.
  - #453: integration users' tokens (VO, GMAO, Giswater, Smartmetering) expire 15 days after being created. **Integration tokens already older than 15 days stop working on deploy**: integrators must log in to get a new one and renew it within 15 days. The GMAO user is identified by `GMAO_USER_USERNAME` or the `gmao` group.
  - #499: `POST /ov/billing-data/update-iban/` and `POST /ov/invoice/paid/` can only be called by the VO account (`ov` group) or a superuser, with attempt limits.
  - #516: the VO answers `400` when it receives the generic ID number `99999999R`.
  - #481: `GET /ov/contract/by-nic/{nic}/` only accepts the 12-digit NIC.
  - #447: the VAT of new or recalculated invoices is calculated on the base of each rate; issued invoices do not change.
  - #510: the GMAO receives the `order.status.changed` event when the application changes an order's status.
  - #498: in the GOT, an operator only sees and assigns themselves their own or unassigned orders.
  - #513: password recovery requires the outgoing email to be configured.
- **Images:** with `CUSTOMERS_VERSION=v1.165.00` in `.env`, `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, then `docker compose exec backend python manage.py migrate --noinput` and `docker compose exec backend python manage.py run_pending_scripts`.
