## Highlights

- General download queue (`ExportJob`) with a **Downloads** menu: exports and the billing register are generated in the background and can be retrieved even after leaving the page
- ACA bonification import from the TXT exchange files (tier extension and social tariff, closures) and ACA files with multiple companies
- OTP document signing (Aqua360 Sign): signed documents retrieved by polling
- Inbound subscriber API for Smart Metering (with OpenAPI) and Virtual Office (OV) API
- Supply cut with forecast and execution as two intervals
- Migration of scanned documents from the source ERP (`import_documents`)
- Person change history, `dni_validated` flag and holder/owner reassignment without subrogation
- New reports: billing by zone, daily activity summary per user, second sheet in the billing register and Incasol report
- SEPA remittances and billing: remittance adjustments, return fee (pricing) and bonification shown on the PDF invoice
- Incidents per cluster and new watchdogs (people without contact, points without meter)
- Fix: the backend now starts on a fresh DB (`billing.0288`/`0289` migrations and `billing/0007` opscript) and `prometeo/reset_db.sh` loads the demo data (`order.OrderReason` fixture)
